\section{Research Plan and Roadmap}
\label{sec:plan}

Table~\ref{tab:roadmap} delineates planned phases and mitigations for RQ3 and RQ4:
\textbf{P1} expands to 100 repos (Java, TS, Python, Go) and 50 JDs/issues to evaluate linguistic generality under mixed-effects ANOVA;
\textbf{P2} injects call-graph/dependency context via precomputed SCIP/LSIF indexes to test whether relational context bridges the semantic gap for AST chunks;
\textbf{P3} executes a Latin-square human verification study with 20 developers measuring requirement verification accuracy, inspection time, and confidence.
